FIRST MATE PHOTO TEST - how to publish on GitHub Pages (about 5 minutes)

1. Go to github.com and sign in. Click + (top right) > New repository.
2. Name it fm-photo-test. Choose Public (required for free Pages). Click Create repository.
3. On the new repo page click "uploading an existing file".
4. Drag in ALL of these files (not the folder): index.html, sw.js, manifest.webmanifest,
   icon-180.png, icon-192.png, icon-512.png. Click Commit changes.
5. Click Settings > Pages (left side). Under "Build and deployment", Source = Deploy from a branch,
   Branch = main, folder = / (root). Click Save.
6. Wait 1-2 minutes. Refresh the Pages screen; it shows the address, like
   https://YOUR-USERNAME.github.io/fm-photo-test/

ON THE IPHONE
1. Open that address in Safari.
2. Tap Share > Add to Home Screen > Add.
3. Open "FM Test" from the home screen icon (not Safari). Setup check should say
   "Running from home screen: Yes". Close and reopen once so "Works offline" says Yes.
4. Take a few photos (try Camera (stays open) and Phone camera), add lines, attach photos.
5. Force close (swipe up), reopen from the icon. Section 3 says Passed or Failed.
6. Repeat in airplane mode, and after restarting the phone.
7. Tap Copy results and paste the text to Claude.

The page holds no company data. Photos never leave the phone in this version.
